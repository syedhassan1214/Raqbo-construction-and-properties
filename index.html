THE IMPORTANT DETAILS

Good questions
deserve clear answers.

Property decisions are big. Here are a few things worth knowing.

Does RAQBO guarantee a property's legal title?

No. Any check should describe its scope and limits. Identity, authority, document reviews and an independent legal opinion are distinct things.

Can I browse without creating an account?

Yes. The intended website allows visitors to explore public listings without signing in. Contacting an advertiser may require a verified account.

Does RAQBO charge commission on direct-owner deals?

The supplied project brief proposes no transaction commission for direct-owner deals. Optional advertising and professional services may have separate disclosed fees.

Does RAQBO handle property deposits or token money?

No. RAQBO does not collect deposits for property sales, hold sale money or provide property escrow. Any future arrangement will start only after legal and banking clearance.
