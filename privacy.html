1. Who we are

RAQBO (“RAQBO”, “we”, “us”) operates the property platform at raqbo.pk, where people in Pakistan can browse, list and enquire about homes, apartments, plots and commercial property, and request property-related services. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your full name, email address and Pakistani mobile number when you create an account. We confirm your email address with a one-time code.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose “Continue with Google”, Google shares your name and email address with us. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Property listings: the details you submit (title, description, sale or rent, property type, advertiser role, city, area, size, price, rooms) and the photos and videos you upload.
  • Service requests and enquiries: your name, phone, optional email, city, property reference, message and preferred contact method.
  • Reports: the reason and details you give when you report a listing, and optional contact details.
  • Saved properties: the listings you mark as favourites.

Information collected automatically

  • Sign-in session data stored in your browser so you stay signed in.
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type and request logs, processed by our hosting, database and security providers to run and protect the service.
  • Security check data: if a security check (Cloudflare Turnstile) is shown, Cloudflare processes signals from your browser to tell people from bots.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive information such as CNIC numbers, bank details or passwords to other services through the website. Please do not include them in listings or messages.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and confirm your email address.
  • To publish your listings after review, show them to visitors, and let you edit or delete them.
  • To review listings, handle reports and keep the platform safe from fraud, spam and misuse.
  • To respond to your service requests and enquiries, including by WhatsApp, phone or email as you choose.
  • To send service messages such as sign-in codes and important updates about your account or listings.
  • To improve the website and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not send marketing messages without your permission.

4. What is shown publicly

When a listing is approved, its details, photos, video, location (area and city) and the advertiser’s name and role (owner, authorized representative or agent) are visible to visitors. Your email address and phone number are not shown publicly. When a visitor contacts an advertiser through RAQBO, the message goes to the RAQBO team, which connects the parties.

6. Where your information is stored

Our service providers may store and process information on servers outside Pakistan. We choose providers that use industry-standard security, and we limit what is shared to what each service needs.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Edit or delete listings at any time from My account. Edited listings go back for review before they reappear.
  • Access or correct your account information by contacting us.
  • Delete your account: email us from your registered address and we will delete your account and personal information, except what we must keep by law.
  • Withdraw consent to being contacted about an enquiry at any time.

We will respond to requests within 30 days.

9. Security

We use access controls so that pending listings, enquiries, reports and contact details are visible only to the people who need them. We use encrypted connections, private file storage and verified sign-in. No online service is completely secure, so please keep your email account safe and contact us immediately if you suspect misuse.

10. Children

RAQBO is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Cookies and browser storage

We use your browser’s storage to keep you signed in, remember saved properties and remember your preferences. We do not use advertising cookies. The security check (Cloudflare Turnstile) and Google sign-in may set their own cookies when used.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as RAQBO grows. We will change the “Last updated” date and, for important changes, notify signed-in users on the website or by email.
